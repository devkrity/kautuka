# Historical channel and workflow plan

This retired plan tracked the early channel attachment and workflow review work. It is kept as a short, sanitised record rather than an active plan; see the [current channel verification guide](../development/channel-verification.md), [Telegram channel UX](../operations/telegram-channel-ux.md), and [workflow documentation](../plans/agent-workflows.md) for maintained guidance.
