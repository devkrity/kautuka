# Support

Kautuka is an early release. When Stable releases are available, the latest Stable release will be the supported baseline for bug reports and security fixes. We will prioritize issues that affect the current Stable release; older Stable releases will receive fixes on a best-effort basis. There is no response-time or maintenance-duration guarantee.

Preview releases are for evaluation. They may have regressions, change compatibility, or be withdrawn from new offers. They are provided on a best-effort basis and are not recommended for production workloads. Preview reports are welcome, but a fix may be delivered in a later Preview or Stable release.

For ordinary product issues, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/devkrity/kautuka/issues) and include the Kautuka version, platform, and a minimal reproduction. Do not include credentials, private documents, or personal data.

For a suspected vulnerability, follow the private reporting instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not post security details in a public issue.
